Export

Excel export is a straight, clean copy of the table above. The Tally export needs your ledger names mapped first, since Tally vouchers post against real ledgers in your Chart of Accounts. The extracted table itself is kept independent of the source bank layout.

Tally XML posts one Payment voucher per debit row and one Receipt voucher per credit row, each pairing your bank ledger against the suspense ledger above. Re-allocate the suspense entries to real expense/income ledgers inside Tally, or edit the ledger name per row before export by adjusting Narration-based rules later.

The extractor detects PDF columns from the statement header and page geometry. Debit/Credit or Amount + Dr/Cr values are read only from their detected amount columns; narration numbers, UTRs, cheque numbers, and other text are not treated as transaction amounts. Running balance is used as a validation check and as a fallback when direction is genuinely ambiguous. Always verify totals against the statement's opening/closing balance before relying on the export.
